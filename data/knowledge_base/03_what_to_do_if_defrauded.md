# What to Do If You Are Defrauded Online

Time is critical. The faster you act, the higher the chance of recovering your money.

## Step 1 — Call 1930 Immediately

**1930** is India's National Cybercrime Helpline. Call it as soon as you realise you have been defrauded. The helpline can work with banks and payment networks to freeze the fraudulent transaction before the money is withdrawn.

Keep this number saved in your phone: **1930**

## Step 2 — Report at cybercrime.gov.in

Visit **https://cybercrime.gov.in** to file an official online cybercrime complaint. You will need:
- Your mobile number and email address
- Details of the fraud (date, time, amount)
- Screenshots or call recordings if available
- The phone number, UPI ID, or bank account of the fraudster (if known)

## Step 3 — Inform Your Bank Immediately

Call your bank's customer care (use the number on the back of your debit/credit card or the bank's official website). Ask them to:
- Block your card or UPI temporarily
- Raise a chargeback or dispute for the fraudulent transaction
- Note the complaint in writing and get a reference number

## Step 4 — File a Police Complaint

Visit your nearest police station or file an online FIR. Bring a printout of your cybercrime complaint and bank statement.

## Step 5 — Gather Evidence

- Take screenshots of all fraudulent messages, calls, and websites.
- Do not delete WhatsApp messages or SMS — they are evidence.
- Write down the exact timeline of events while it is fresh.

## What NOT to Do

- Do NOT share any more OTP, PIN, or personal details with anyone claiming to help "recover" your money — this is often a second scam targeting fraud victims.
- Do NOT pay anyone upfront to "recover" lost money.

## Recovery of Money

Recovery is not guaranteed, but acting within the first few hours significantly improves the chances. RBI has guidelines requiring banks to take prompt action on cybercrime complaints.
