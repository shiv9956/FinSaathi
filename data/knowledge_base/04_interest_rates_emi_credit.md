# Understanding Interest Rates, EMI, and Credit Score

## Simple Interest vs Compound Interest

**Simple Interest (SI)** is calculated only on the original principal.
- Formula: Interest = Principal × Rate × Time
- Example: ₹10,000 at 10% per year for 2 years = ₹2,000 interest.
- Used in: some government savings schemes, personal loans quoted as "flat rate".

**Compound Interest (CI)** is calculated on the principal plus accumulated interest.
- Your interest earns interest over time.
- Used in: savings accounts, FDs, home loans, credit card debt.
- Compounding frequency matters — monthly compounding grows faster than annual.

**Key takeaway:** Compound interest works in your favour when you are saving (your money grows faster), but against you when you are borrowing (your debt grows faster).

## APR vs Flat Rate

**APR (Annual Percentage Rate)** represents the true yearly cost of a loan, including fees and interest. Use APR to compare loans.

**Flat rate** calculates interest on the full principal for the entire tenure, even as you repay. A "12% flat rate" loan is much more expensive than a "12% reducing-balance" loan.

When comparing loan offers, always ask for the APR or "effective interest rate."

## EMI Basics

**EMI (Equated Monthly Instalment)** is the fixed monthly payment you make to repay a loan. Each EMI has two parts:
1. **Interest component** — higher in early months, decreases over time.
2. **Principal component** — lower in early months, increases over time.

This is called a **reducing-balance** (or amortisation) schedule.

Use an EMI calculator to understand your monthly commitment before taking a loan. Ensure the EMI does not exceed 30-40% of your monthly income (this is a general guideline, not a rule).

## What is a Credit Score?

A credit score (CIBIL score in India) is a 3-digit number (300–900) that tells lenders how reliably you repay debt.

| Score Range | Rating |
|---|---|
| 750–900 | Excellent — best loan rates |
| 650–749 | Good — most loans approved |
| 550–649 | Fair — higher interest rates |
| Below 550 | Poor — loan approval difficult |

**How to improve your score:**
- Pay all EMIs and credit card bills on time.
- Keep credit card usage below 30% of your limit.
- Do not apply for many loans at once.
- Check your credit report once a year for errors (free at CIBIL, Experian, CRIF, Equifax).

## Safe Interest Rates for Loans

Typical ranges vary by bank and market conditions — always check current rates on your bank's website. As a rough guide:
- Home loans: lower rates, usually secured
- Personal loans: higher rates than home loans
- Credit card debt: highest rates — pay in full each month to avoid interest

Rates change over time. Check the RBI website for current lending rate benchmarks: https://www.rbi.org.in
