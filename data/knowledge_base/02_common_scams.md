# Common UPI and Online Scams in India

Fraudsters use many tricks to steal money through UPI and online banking. Knowing these tricks is the best protection.

## 1. Fake KYC Expiry Scam

**How it works:** You get an SMS or WhatsApp message saying "Your KYC is expired. Click here to update or your account will be blocked." The link takes you to a fake website that steals your login details or installs malware.

**Reality:** Banks and the RBI do NOT send links to update KYC through SMS. If you need to update KYC, visit your bank branch or the bank's official website directly.

## 2. OTP and Vishing Scam

**How it works:** A caller pretends to be a bank employee, NPCI officer, or government official. They say there is a problem with your account and ask you to share the OTP you just received on your phone.

**Reality:** No bank, government body, or legitimate company will EVER ask for your OTP. If you share your OTP, the fraudster completes a transaction from your account.

## 3. QR Code "Receive Money" Trick

**How it works:** Someone says they want to pay you money and asks you to "scan this QR code to receive it." When you scan and enter your UPI PIN, money is actually sent OUT of your account.

**Reality:** Scanning a QR code and entering your UPI PIN SENDS money. You never need a PIN to receive money.

## 4. Fake Customer-Care Numbers

**How it works:** Fraudsters post fake helpline numbers on Google or social media for banks, UPI apps, or e-commerce sites. When you call, they ask for card details, OTP, or remote access to your phone.

**Reality:** Find customer-care numbers only on the official bank or app website. Never trust numbers found on search engines.

## 5. Phishing Links

**How it works:** You receive an email or SMS with a link that looks like your bank's website. The fake site steals your username and password.

**Reality:** Always type your bank's URL directly. Check the URL starts with https:// and shows the correct bank name.

## 6. Loan App Scams

**How it works:** Fake loan apps offer instant loans and then demand upfront "processing fees" or "insurance deposits." After taking the fee, they disappear.

**Reality:** Legitimate lenders deduct fees from the loan amount. Never pay money before receiving a loan.

## 7. Job and Work-From-Home Scams

**How it works:** A fake job posting offers high pay for easy work from home. After "joining," you are asked to pay a registration fee, training fee, or buy a "kit."

**Reality:** No legitimate employer charges you money to hire you.

## 8. Prize and Lottery Scams

**How it works:** A call or message says you have won a prize, lottery, or KBC reward. To claim it, you must pay "taxes" or "processing fees" first.

**Reality:** You cannot win a lottery you never entered. There is no free prize that requires upfront payment.

## Stay Safe

- Trust your instincts — if something feels wrong, it usually is.
- When in doubt, hang up and call the official number.
- Report all scams at https://cybercrime.gov.in or call 1930.
