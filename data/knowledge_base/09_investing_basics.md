# Responsible Investing — What Every Beginner Should Know

This guide is for educational purposes only. It does not recommend any specific investment. For personalised advice, consult a SEBI-registered investment advisor.

## Basic Principles of Investing

1. **Start with an emergency fund.** Never invest money you might need in the next 3-6 months.
2. **Understand before you invest.** If you cannot explain how an investment works to someone else, do not invest in it.
3. **Diversify.** Do not put all your money in one place.
4. **Beware of "guaranteed high returns."** No legitimate investment can guarantee very high fixed returns with no risk.
5. **Time in the market matters more than timing the market** — regular, long-term investing generally outperforms trying to predict market movements.

## Common Investment Products (Overview Only)

**Savings Account / FD / RD / PPF:** Safe, regulated. Returns are modest but predictable. Suitable for beginners and emergency funds.

**Mutual Funds:** Pool of money managed by a professional fund manager. Returns are not guaranteed and vary with market conditions. Types include equity, debt, and hybrid funds. Regulated by SEBI.

**Stocks / Shares:** Ownership in a company. High potential returns but also high risk. Requires research and understanding of the business. Regulated by SEBI.

**Gold:** Traditional store of value. Can be held physically, in ETFs, or in Sovereign Gold Bonds.

**Real Estate:** Long-term investment in property. High capital required. Not easily liquidated.

**Cryptocurrency:** Highly speculative. Not regulated by RBI or SEBI. Very high risk. Only invest what you can afford to lose completely.

## Warning Signs of Investment Fraud

- "Guaranteed" returns of 30%, 50%, or more per year.
- Pressure to invest quickly ("limited time offer").
- Asking you to recruit friends or family (multi-level marketing structures).
- No transparent records of where your money is invested.
- No registration number from SEBI or RBI.

Always verify a financial advisor's SEBI registration at https://www.sebi.gov.in before giving them money.

## What Beginners Should Do First

1. Open a bank savings account.
2. Build an emergency fund (3-6 months of expenses in FD or savings).
3. Get basic insurance (health insurance).
4. Then explore mutual funds or PPF for long-term goals.
