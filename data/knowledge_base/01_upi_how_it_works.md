# How UPI Works and How to Pay Safely

## What is UPI?

UPI (Unified Payments Interface) is a real-time digital payment system built by the National Payments Corporation of India (NPCI). It lets you send and receive money instantly using your smartphone, 24 hours a day, 7 days a week — including bank holidays.

UPI works by linking your bank account to a Virtual Payment Address (VPA), also called a UPI ID. Examples: yourname@oksbi, yourname@ybl, yourname@paytm.

## How to Make a UPI Payment

1. Open any UPI app on your phone (BHIM, PhonePe, Google Pay, Paytm, etc.).
2. Choose "Send Money" or "Pay".
3. Enter the recipient's UPI ID, mobile number, or scan their QR code.
4. Enter the amount.
5. Review the recipient's name carefully.
6. Enter your **UPI PIN** to confirm.
7. Done — the money transfers instantly.

## How to Receive Money

To receive money, simply share your UPI ID or QR code with the sender. **You do NOT need to enter your UPI PIN to receive money.** If someone tells you to enter your PIN to receive money, it is a scam.

## Safety Rules for UPI Payments

- **Never share your UPI PIN** with anyone — not even bank staff, UPI app support, or family.
- **Always check the recipient's name** before confirming payment.
- **Verify the QR code** — scan QR codes only from trusted merchants. A sticker placed over the original QR code is a common fraud.
- **Do not click links** in SMS or WhatsApp to "collect" or "receive" money — these are scams.
- **Set a daily limit** in your UPI app settings to limit damage if your phone is stolen.
- Use only official UPI apps downloaded from the Google Play Store or Apple App Store.
- If your phone is lost, immediately call your bank to block your UPI access.

## What is a UPI Collect Request?

A collect request is sent by someone asking you to approve a payment from your account. **Approving a collect request sends money OUT of your account.** Never approve a collect request from an unknown person.

## UPI Transaction Limits

Limits vary by bank and app. Check your bank's website for current limits. Most banks allow multiple transactions per day up to a daily cap.
